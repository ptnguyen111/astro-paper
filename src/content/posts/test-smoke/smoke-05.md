### READING (16 points)

**Read the following passage and answer the questions.**

**The Resilient Zone: Building Strong Feelings**

In our lives, we all have many feelings. These feelings can be sorted into different groups. The "green zone" is when we feel good, comfortable or excited. The "yellow zone" is when we feel a little worried or upset. For instance, the moment before having a hard test or when we are starving. Finally, you are in the "red zone" when you feel extremely uncomfortable, upset, angry, or scared of something. This happens when you are bullied, experience shocking events, or are very tired and exhausted.

It's normal to experience different feelings or move through different emotional zones throughout the day, but it's very important to know how to go back to the "green zone", the place that makes you feel happy, peaceful, calm, motivated and ready to achieve anything. This is called our "resilient zone." It means we have a safe place inside us where we can take a deep breath, calm down, and get better when things are hard.

We can learn to find our resilient zone in many ways. We can try breathing slowly, thinking of good things, or talking to someone we trust. Parents, teachers, and other grown-ups can help you understand your emotions and feel better.

Everyone is different. So, we need to find out what helps us feel good again when we are feeling bad. Some kids like to listen to music, draw pictures, or play with their pets.

Being resilient doesn't mean we never feel bad. It means we know how to deal with our bad feelings and become strong again. It's like having a superpower that helps us do well when things are hard and stay happy.
