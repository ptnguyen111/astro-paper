## I. SCIENCE — Questions 1–6

### Question 1
> Ocean tides (thuỷ triều) rise and fall approximately twice per day. Although the Sun also exerts gravitational force on Earth's oceans, the Moon has a much stronger influence on tides. Why does the Moon affect tides more than the Sun, even though the Sun is far more massive?

**A**. The Moon is made of water, which attracts ocean water \
**B**. The Moon is much closer to Earth, so its gravitational pull has a stronger effect despite its smaller mass \
**C**. The Sun only affects tides during the daytime \
**D**. The Moon's magnetic field drags ocean water toward it

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: B.</b> The Moon is much closer to Earth, so its gravitational pull has a stronger tidal effect despite its smaller mass. Tidal force depends on the gradient of gravity, which falls off with the cube of distance — not just the square.</i>
</details>

### Question 2
> In a food web, organisms are classified by how they obtain energy. Which of the following sequences correctly represents the flow of energy from one trophic level to the next?

**A**. Carnivore → Herbivore → Producer \
**B**. Producer → Primary Consumer → Secondary Consumer \
**C**. Decomposer → Producer → Predator \
**D**. Omnivore → Herbivore → Carnivore

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: B.</b> Energy flows from producers (plants) → primary consumers (herbivores) → secondary consumers (carnivores). This is the correct trophic level sequence in a food web.</i>
</details>

### Question 3
> The respiratory system (hệ hô hấp) is responsible for gas exchange in the human body. Which statement accurately describes how oxygen travels from the air into the bloodstream?

**A**. Oxygen enters the nose, dissolves in stomach acid, and is absorbed by blood vessels in the stomach \
**B**. Oxygen passes through the trachea into the lungs, enters tiny air sacs called alveoli, and diffuses into surrounding capillaries \
**C**. Oxygen is carried by white blood cells directly from the mouth to the heart \
**D**. Oxygen moves from the lungs into the liver, which distributes it to the rest of the body

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: B.</b> Oxygen enters via the nose/mouth, passes through the trachea into the lungs, enters tiny air sacs called alveoli, and diffuses across thin capillary walls into the bloodstream.</i>
</details>

### Question 4
> Igneous rocks (đá magma) are formed when molten magma from beneath Earth's crust cools and solidifies. However, sedimentary rocks such as limestone are formed differently. Which process best describes the formation of sedimentary rocks?

**A**. Molten rock erupts from a volcano and cools instantly upon contact with air \
**B**. Layers of sand, mud, and organic material accumulate over millions of years and are compressed into solid rock \
**C**. Existing rocks are melted by intense heat from Earth's core and reform into new crystals \
**D**. Metamorphic rocks are weathered by wind and water until they become smooth pebbles

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: B.</b> Sedimentary rocks form when layers of sand, mud, and organic material accumulate over millions of years and are compressed into solid rock (compaction and cementation).</i>
</details>

### Question 5
> Scientists in 2025 announced that more than 16,000 new species are now being identified each year — far more than previously estimated. Why does the passage of time and technological advancement make it easier for scientists to discover new species?

**A**. Animals are evolving faster than ever before \
**B**. Climate change is creating brand-new animals that did not previously exist \
**C**. Better DNA analysis tools allow scientists to distinguish species that look identical but are genetically different \

<details name="question-5">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>

### Question 6
> A student places a green plant in a sealed transparent container with a sensor that measures carbon dioxide (CO₂) levels. She places the container in a dark cupboard for 24 hours. She observes that the CO₂ level has increased. She then moves the container to a sunny windowsill for another 24 hours and observes that the CO₂ level has decreased. Which explanation best accounts for both observations?

**A**. In darkness, the plant produced CO₂ through respiration; in sunlight, photosynthesis consumed CO₂ faster than respiration produced it \
**B**. The plant died in the dark cupboard and decomposed, releasing CO₂ \
**C**. Sunlight directly converts CO₂ into oxygen without the plant's involvement \
**D**. The sensor was faulty in the dark and only worked correctly in sunlight

<details name="question-6">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: A.</b> In darkness, the plant respires (producing CO₂). In sunlight, photosynthesis consumes CO₂ at a faster rate than respiration produces it, so CO₂ levels drop.</i>
</details>

### Question 10

### Question 12
> A farmer harvested 480 kilograms of rice. He sold 2/5 of the rice to a merchant and 1/4 of the rice to a local market. How many kilograms of rice does he have remaining?

**A**. 120 kg \
**B**. 156 kg \
**C**. 168 kg \
**D**. 204 kg

<details name="question-12">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: C.</b> Total = 480 kg. Sold: 2/5 × 480 = 192 kg (merchant). 1/4 × 480 = 120 kg (market). Total sold = 312 kg. Remaining = 480 − 312 = <b>168 kg</b>.</i>
</details>

### Question 13
> A train departs from Hà Nội at 6:45 AM and arrives in Hải Phòng at 8:15 AM. If the distance between the two cities is 108 kilometres, what is the average speed of the train in km/h?

**A**. 54 km/h \
**B**. 60 km/h \
**C**. 72 km/h \
**D**. 84 km/h

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: C.</b> Travel time = 6:45 AM → 8:15 AM = 1.5 hours. Speed = 108 km / 1.5 h = <b>72 km/h</b>.</i>
</details>

### Question 14
> In a bag, there are red marbles and blue marbles in a ratio of 3:5. If there are 24 red marbles, how many marbles are there in total?

**A**. 40 marbles \
**B**. 48 marbles \
**C**. 56 marbles \
**D**. 64 marbles

<details name="question-14">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: D.</b> Ratio red:blue = 3:5. Red = 24 = 3 parts → 1 part = 8. Blue = 5 × 8 = 40. Total = 24 + 40 = <b>64 marbles</b>.</i>
</details>

### Question 15
> A rectangle measures 12 cm by 8 cm. A smaller square is cut out from one corner of the rectangle. The square has a side length of 4 cm. What is the perimeter of the remaining shape?

**A**. 32 cm \
**B**. 36 cm \
**C**. 40 cm \
**D**. 44 cm

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: C.</b> Original perimeter = 2(12+8) = 40 cm. Cutting a 4×4 square from a corner removes two 4 cm edges but adds two new 4 cm edges inside the cut. Net change = 0. Perimeter stays <b>40 cm</b>.</i>
</details>

### Question 16
> Bình is thinking of a number. If you add 15 to the number and then multiply the result by 4, you get 100. What is Bình's original number?

**A**. 5 \
**B**. 10 \
**C**. 15 \
**D**. 20

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><b>Đáp án: B.</b> Let number = x. (x + 15) × 4 = 100 → x + 15 = 25 → x = <b>10</b>.</i>
</details>

### Question 18

>
> ```
> Nhưng còn cần cho trẻ

> Để bế bồng chăm sóc
>

> Thế là bố sinh ra
> Bố bảo cho biết ngoan
> Bố dạy cho biết nghĩ.
> ```
>
> *(Trích "Chuyện cổ tích về loài người", Xuân Quỳnh)*
>
> Theo đoạn thơ trên, lí do mẹ và bố được sinh ra là gì?

**A**. Mẹ sinh ra để làm việc nhà, bố sinh ra để kiếm tiền nuôi gia đình \
**B**. Mẹ sinh ra để yêu thương, chăm sóc trẻ; bố sinh ra để dạy dỗ, cho trẻ hiểu biết \
**C**. Mẹ sinh ra để hát ru, bố sinh ra để kể chuyện cổ tích \
**D**. Mẹ sinh ra trước bố, và bố sinh ra sau để phụ giúp mẹ

<details name="question-18">
