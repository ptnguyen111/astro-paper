## I. SCIENCE

### Question 1

> Pasta is made from wheat, containing mostly starch. Where does starch digestion primarily begin in the digestive system?

**A**. Mouth — enzymes in saliva break starch into simpler sugars \
**B**. Stomach — acid breaks down starch \
**C**. Small intestine — bile digests starch \
**D**. Large intestine — water decomposes starch

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Tiêu hóa tinh bột bắt đầu ở miệng, nơi enzyme amylase trong nước bọt phân giải tinh bột thành đường đơn.</i>
</details>

### Question 2

> Thalidomide, a 1950s drug, contained both left-handed and right-handed versions. One treated morning sickness; the other caused birth defects. What does this demonstrate?

**A**. All drugs are equally dangerous \
**B**. The spatial arrangement of atoms determines whether a substance is helpful or harmful \
**C**. Biological molecules are not affected by chirality \
**D**. Mirror-image molecules always have identical effects

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Thảm họa thalidomide chứng minh sự sắp xếp không gian của nguyên tử (chirality) quyết định chất có lợi hay có hại.</i>
</details>

### Question 3

> Most crabs inherited their sideways walk from a single ancestor ~200 million years ago. What does this best demonstrate about evolution?

**A**. Each crab species independently learned to walk sideways \
**B**. Sideways walking requires more energy \
**C**. The trait emerged once in an ancient ancestor and was passed down to all descendants \
**D**. Crabs switched due to climate change

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Đặc điểm đi ngang xuất hiện một lần ở tổ tiên chung và được di truyền bởi tất cả loài cua hậu duệ.</i>
</details>

### Question 4

> The gene for attached earlobes is recessive. Both parents have free-hanging earlobes but carry one recessive copy each. Probability their child has attached earlobes?

**A**. 0% \
**B**. 25% \
**C**. 50% \
**D**. 100%

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Cả bố mẹ đều dị hợp tử (Rr) → Punnett: RR(25%), Rr(50%), rr(25%). Xác suất dái tai dính (rr) = 25%.</i>
</details>

### Question 5

> Cacti have sharp spines instead of leaves and thick fleshy stems. Which statement correctly explains these adaptations?

**A**. Spines attract insects; stem stores CO₂ \
**B**. Spines perform photosynthesis; stem stores water \
**C**. Spines minimize water loss; thick stem stores water for dry periods \
**D**. Spines protect from animals; thin stem maximizes heat absorption

<details name="question-5">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Gai (lá biến đổi) giảm diện tích bề mặt → giảm mất nước; thân dày dự trữ nước cho thời kỳ khô hạn.</i>
</details>

### Question 6

> A student rubs a magnet along a steel needle 50 times. The needle floats on a cork and rotates to point north-south. What principle does this demonstrate?

**A**. Water conducts electricity \
**B**. Iron-rich materials can be magnetized and align with Earth's magnetic field \
**C**. Cork neutralizes magnetic properties \
**D**. Rubbing metal against cork produces static electricity

<details name="question-6">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Cọ xát nam châm từ hóa cây kim → kim trở thành la bàn, căn chỉnh theo từ trường Trái Đất.</i>
</details>

### Question 15

> Sequence: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ? What is the next number?

**A**. 44 \
**B**. 46 \
**C**. 48 \
**D**. 50

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — n²−1: 2²−1=3, 3²−1=8, 4²−1=15, 5²−1=24, 6²−1=35, 7²−1=48.</i>
</details>

### Question 16

> A 24 cm × 16 cm paper is folded in half twice. What is the area of each folded section?

**A**. 48 cm² \
**B**. 96 cm² \
**C**. 192 cm² \
**D**. 384 cm²

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Diện tích ban đầu = 384 cm². Gấp đôi hai lần = 4 phần. Mỗi phần = 384/4 = 96 cm².</i>
</details>

### Question 17

> A clear cube has edges of 6 cm. An insect is exactly at the centre. How far must it travel to reach the nearest corner?

**A**. 3 cm \
**B**. 3√3 cm \
**C**. 6 cm \
**D**. 6√2 cm

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Từ tâm đến góc = nửa đường chéo không gian = √(3²+3²+3²) = √27 = 3√3 cm.</i>
</details>
