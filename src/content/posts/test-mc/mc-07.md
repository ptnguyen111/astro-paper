### Question 1
> Pasta is made from wheat (lúa mì). It contains mostly starch (tinh bột) with some protein. When a person eats a bowl of pasta, the starch digestion primarily begins in which part of the digestive system?

**A**. The mouth, where enzymes in saliva break starch into simpler sugars \
**B**. The stomach, where acid immediately breaks down starch \
**C**. The small intestine, where bile from the liver digests starch \
**D**. The large intestine, where water helps decompose starch

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: A — Starch digestion primarily begins in the mouth, where the enzyme amylase in saliva breaks starch down into simpler sugars. The stomach uses acid (not for starch), the small intestine continues digestion, and the large intestine absorbs water.</i>
</details>

### Question 2

One side treated morning sickness; the other caused severe birth defects. This tragedy demonstrates that:

</details>

### Question 4
> A child receives one set of genes from their mother and one from their father. The gene for attached earlobes (dái tai dính) is recessive (lặn), while the gene for free-hanging earlobes is dominant (trội). If both parents have free-hanging earlobes but each carries one copy of the recessive attached-earlobe gene, what is the probability that their child will have attached earlobes?

**A**. 0% — impossible, since both parents have free earlobes \
**B**. 25% — the child could inherit the recessive gene from both parents \
**C**. 50% — one parent will pass on the recessive gene \
**D**. 100% — the child will definitely have attached earlobes

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: B — Both parents are heterozygous (Rr), carrying one dominant free-earlobe allele and one recessive attached-earlobe allele. Punnett square: RR (25%), Rr (50%), rr (25%). Probability of attached earlobes (rr) = 25%.</i>
</details>

### Question 5
> Cacti are plants that thrive in extremely dry deserts. Unlike most plants, cacti have converted their leaves into sharp spines (gai) and their stems have become thick and fleshy. Which statement correctly explains the function of these adaptations?

**A**. Spines attract insects for pollination, while the stem stores carbon dioxide \
**B**. Spines perform photosynthesis since cacti have no stems, and the thick stems store water \
**C**. Spines reduce water loss by minimizing surface area, while the thick stem stores water for dry periods \
**D**. Spines protect the cactus from animals, while the thin stem maximizes heat absorption

<details name="question-5">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: C — Cactus spines (modified leaves) have minimal surface area to reduce water loss through transpiration, while the thick, fleshy stem stores water for long dry periods — both critical adaptations for desert survival.</i>
</details>

### Question 14
> A shopkeeper increases the price of a book by 20%. However, nobody buys it, so he then decreases the new price by 20%. The final price compared to the original price is:

**A**. 4% lower than the original \
**B**. The same as the original \
**C**. 4% higher than the original \
**D**. 20% lower than the original

<details name="question-14">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: A — Let original price = P. After +20%: P×1.2 = 1.2P. After −20%: 1.2P×0.8 = 0.96P, which is 4% lower than the original.</i>

### Question 15
> Look at this sequence of numbers: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?
> What is the next number in this sequence?

**A**. 44 \
**B**. 46 \
**C**. 48 \
**D**. 50

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: C — The sequence follows n²−1: 2²−1=3, 3²−1=8, 4²−1=15, 5²−1=24, 6²−1=35, 7²−1=48. Alternatively, differences increase by 2 each time: +5, +7, +9, +11, +13 → 35+13=48.</i>
</details>
