## I. SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY

### Question 1
> A scientist finds trilobite fossils in one rock layer and ammonite fossils in another layer far away. We know that trilobites died out about 252 million years ago, while ammonites survived until 66 million years ago. What can the scientist conclude about the two rock layers?

**A**. They are the same age because both contain sea animal fossils. \
**B**. The layer with trilobites is older than the layer with ammonites. \
**C**. They cannot be compared without measuring radioactive elements in the rocks. \
**D**. The layer with trilobites is younger because trilobites evolved later.

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Trilobites went extinct at the Permian-Triassic boundary (~252 mya), while ammonites persisted until the K-Pg extinction (~66 mya). Therefore the trilobite-bearing stratum is older.</i>
</details>

### Question 2
> The Gulf Stream carries warm water from the Caribbean toward Europe. Because of this current, countries like England and France have much warmer winters than places at the same latitude in Canada. How does the Gulf Stream warm Europe?

**A**. The current creates strong winds that push cold air away from Europe. \
**B**. Warm water releases heat into the air above it, and winds carry this warm air to Europe. \
**C**. The salt in the current absorbs heat from the Sun and holds it longer. \
**D**. Warm water sinks deep in the ocean, then resurfaces near Europe releasing stored heat.

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — The Gulf Stream releases thermal energy via sensible heat flux; prevailing westerlies carry this warmed air across Europe.</i>
</details>

### Question 3
> A gardener grows tomatoes using hydroponics (growing plants in water instead of soil). The tomato leaves turn yellow and the roots stay small, even though the gardener adds plant food regularly. Testing shows the water has pH 8.2 (too alkaline) and very low electrical conductivity (too few dissolved nutrients). Why are the plants unhealthy?

**A**. The high pH locks up iron so the plants can't absorb it, and there are not enough nutrients in the water for the roots to grow properly. \
**B**. The high pH dissolves the roots' outer layer, and the low nutrient level poisons the water inside the plant stems. \
**C**. The alkaline water creates mold that eats the roots, and the low conductivity means the water is too pure for any plant to survive. \
**D**. The high pH turns the nutrients into gas that escapes into the air, and the low conductivity makes the water freeze inside the plant.

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — High pH (8.2) causes iron (Fe³⁺) to precipitate as insoluble hydroxides, making it unavailable. Low EC indicates insufficient nutrients.</i>
</details>

### Question 4
> A vaccine teaches the body to fight a virus. When someone gets a vaccine against COVID-19, their body learns to make special cells that remember the virus for years. What is the correct sequence of events inside the body after receiving the vaccine?

**A**. Virus protein enters → white blood cells show it to helper cells → helper cells activate B cells → B cells make antibodies and memory cells. \
**B**. Virus protein enters → B cells immediately make antibodies → antibodies stay in the blood forever without needing memory cells. \
**C**. Virus protein enters → skin cells absorb it → skin becomes stronger against future viruses. \
**D**. Virus protein enters → red blood cells carry it to the heart → the heart pumps out the virus.

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Canonical adaptive immune response: APCs (MHC II) → CD4⁺ T cells → B cell activation → germinal center affinity maturation → memory B cells + long-lived plasma cells.</i>
</details>

### Question 5
> The Moon takes about 27.3 days to orbit Earth (relative to the stars), but it takes about 29.5 days to go from one full Moon to the next full Moon. Why is there a 2-day difference?

**A**. Because the Moon's orbit is tilted, making it travel a longer path between full Moons. \
**B**. Because while the Moon orbits Earth, Earth also moves along its orbit around the Sun, so the Moon needs extra time to catch up to the same Earth-Sun-Moon alignment. \
**C**. Because the Moon's orbit is oval-shaped, causing it to slow down between full Moons. \
**D**. Because the Moon's orbit slowly rotates backward each month, adding extra travel distance.

<details name="question-5">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — During a lunar month, Earth advances ~30° along its orbit. The Moon must travel this extra angular distance to realign with the Earth-Sun line, making the synodic period (29.5 days) longer than the sidereal period (27.3 days).</i>
</details>

### Question 6
> You see a lightning flash, but you hear the thunder about 5 seconds later for every 1.6 km away the lightning struck. Why do you see the lightning before you hear the thunder?

**A**. Light travels much faster than sound. Light moves at 300,000 km/s while sound moves at only about 0.34 km/s through air. \
**B**. Your eyes react faster than your ears. The brain processes visual signals before sound signals. \
**C**. Lightning is much brighter than thunder is loud, so the flash reaches you more easily. \
**D**. Sound waves fade away quickly over distance, so you only hear thunder after enough sound builds up.

<details name="question-6">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Light (electromagnetic wave) travels at ~3×10⁸ m/s; sound (mechanical compression wave) at ~340 m/s through air.</i>
</details>

**C**. The park is surrounded by coffee farms, dams, and roads, making it an isolated island of forest — too small for large animals to survive long-term without inbreeding. \

### Question 11
> A solid figure is formed by placing a half-cylinder on top of a rectangular prism. The rectangular prism has dimensions 36 cm × 24 cm × 48 cm. The half-cylinder sits on the top face of the prism, with its length equal to the prism's length (36 cm) and its diameter equal to the prism's width (24 cm). What is the total surface area of the composite solid?

**A**. (6624 + 576π) cm² \
**B**. (7488 + 576π) cm² \
**C**. (6624 + 1152π) cm² \
**D**. (7488 + 1152π) cm²

<details name="question-11">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Prism SA (without top) = 2(36×24 + 36×48 + 24×48) − 36×24 = 7488 − 864 = 6624. Half-cylinder curved = π×12×36 = 432π. Two half-circles = π×12² = 144π. Total = 6624 + 576π.</i>
</details>

### Question 14
> 720 mL of a 25% salt solution is mixed with 480 mL of a 50% salt solution. What is the salt concentration of the resulting mixture?

**A**. 35% \
**B**. 37.5% \
**C**. 40% \
**D**. 42%

<details name="question-14">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Salt: 720×0.25 + 480×0.50 = 180 + 240 = 420g. Total: 720+480 = 1200mL. Conc: 420/1200 = 35%.</i>
</details>

### Question 15
> Three pipes are connected to a tank. Pipe A can fill the tank in 8 hours. Pipe B can fill the tank in 12 hours. Pipe C can drain the tank in 24 hours. If all three pipes are opened simultaneously, how long will it take to fill the tank?

**A**. 6 hours \
**B**. 4 hours 48 minutes \
**C**. 4 hours \
**D**. 3 hours

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Combined rate = 1/8 + 1/12 − 1/24 = 3/24 + 2/24 − 1/24 = 4/24 = 1/6. Time = 6 hours.</i>
</details>

### Question 16
> A two-digit number has a tens digit that is 3 less than twice its units digit. The sum of the number and the number formed by reversing its digits is 132. What is the original number?

**A**. 75 \
**B**. 93 \
**C**. 57 \
**D**. 84

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Let u = units, t = 2u−3 = tens. Number = 10(2u−3)+u = 21u−30. Reverse = 10u+2u−3 = 12u−3. Sum = 33u−33 = 132 → u=5, t=7. Number = 75.</i>
</details>
