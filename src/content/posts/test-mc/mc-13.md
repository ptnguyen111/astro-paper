## I. SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY

### Question 1
> Trilobite fossils in one layer (died out ~252 mya), ammonite fossils in another (survived until ~66 mya). What can be concluded?

**A**. Same age (both sea animals) \
**B**. Trilobite layer is older \
**C**. Cannot compare without radiometric dating \
**D**. Trilobite layer is younger

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Bọ ba thùy tuyệt chủng ~252 triệu năm trước; ammonite tồn tại đến ~66 triệu năm trước → lớp có trilobite già hơn.</i>
</details>

### Question 2
> The Gulf Stream carries warm water from the Caribbean toward Europe, giving England/France warmer winters than Canada at the same latitude. How?

**A**. Creates strong winds pushing cold air away \
**B**. Warm water releases heat into the air above; winds carry warm air to Europe \
**C**. Salt in the current absorbs heat longer \
**D**. Warm water sinks, resurfaces near Europe

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Gulf Stream giải phóng nhiệt; gió tây ôn đới mang không khí ấm đến châu Âu.</i>
</details>

### Question 3
> Hydroponic tomatoes: leaves turn yellow, roots stay small. Water has pH 8.2 (too alkaline) and very low EC (too few nutrients). Why unhealthy?

**A**. High pH locks up iron; too few nutrients for roots \
**B**. High pH dissolves root outer layer \
**C**. Alkaline water creates mold eating roots \
**D**. High pH turns nutrients into gas

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — pH cao (8,2) làm sắt kết tủa không hòa tan; EC thấp cho thấy không đủ dinh dưỡng.</i>
</details>

### Question 4
> A vaccine teaches the body to fight a virus. Correct sequence of events after vaccination?

**A**. Virus protein → white blood cells show it to helper cells → helper cells activate B cells → B cells make antibodies + memory cells \
**B**. Virus protein → B cells immediately make antibodies → antibodies stay forever \
**C**. Virus protein → skin cells absorb it → skin becomes stronger \
**D**. Virus protein → red blood cells carry to heart → heart pumps it out

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Đáp ứng miễn dịch thích ứng: APC → Tế bào T CD4⁺ → Kích hoạt tế bào B → tế bào B nhớ + tế bào plasma.</i>
</details>

### Question 5
> The Moon takes 27.3 days to orbit Earth but 29.5 days between full Moons. Why the ~2 day difference?

**A**. Tilted orbit makes longer path \
**B**. Earth also moves along its orbit around the Sun; the Moon needs extra time to catch up to the same alignment \
**C**. Oval-shaped orbit slows it down \
**D**. Orbit rotates backward

<details name="question-5">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Trái Đất tiến ~30° dọc quỹ đạo trong 1 tháng → Mặt Trăng cần thêm thời gian để thẳng hàng trở lại.</i>
</details>

### Question 6
> You see lightning before hearing thunder ~5 seconds apart per 1.6 km distance. Why?

**A**. Light (300,000 km/s) travels much faster than sound (~0.34 km/s) \
**B**. Eyes react faster than ears \
**C**. Lightning is brighter than thunder is loud \
**D**. Sound fades over distance

<details name="question-6">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Ánh sáng (sóng điện từ) ~3×10⁸ m/s; âm thanh (sóng nén) ~340 m/s trong không khí.</i>
</details>

### Question 8
> Cát Tiên National Park (72,000 ha) is Vietnam's largest lowland rainforest. Why does it still face serious long-term risk?

**A**. All at same elevation — animals can't escape climate change uphill \
**B**. No rivers running through \

**D**. Government allows logging inside

</details>

### Question 11
> A solid: rectangular prism (36×24×48 cm) with a half-cylinder on top (length=36, diameter=24). Total surface area?

**A**. (6624 + 576π) cm² \
**B**. (7488 + 576π) cm² \
**C**. (6624 + 1152π) cm² \
**D**. (7488 + 1152π) cm²

<details name="question-11">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Lăng trụ (không mặt trên)=6624. Nửa trụ cong=432π. Hai nửa tròn=144π. Tổng=6624+576π.</i>
</details>

### Question 12
> Bag: 10 red, 6 blue marbles. Draw 4 without replacement. Probability of exactly 3 red and 1 blue?

**A**. 36/91 \
**B**. 135/364 \
**C**. 30/91 \
**D**. 3/26

<details name="question-12">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — C(10,3)×C(6,1)÷C(16,4) = 120×6÷1820 = 720/1820 = 36/91.</i>
</details>

### Question 15
> 3 pipes: A fills in 8h, B fills in 12h, C drains in 24h. All opened simultaneously. Time to fill?

**A**. 6 hours \
**B**. 4h48m \
**C**. 4 hours \
**D**. 3 hours

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — 1/8+1/12−1/24 = 3/24+2/24−1/24 = 4/24 = 1/6. TG=6 giờ.</i>
</details>
