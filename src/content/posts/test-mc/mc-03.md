### Question 1
> The brain and spinal cord together form the central nervous system (CNS). The spinal cord runs down your back inside the vertebral column. If a person injures their spinal cord, they may lose the ability to move their limbs despite having a perfectly healthy brain. Which of the following best explains this phenomenon?

**A**. The spinal cord stores memories about how to walk and move, so injury destroys those memories. \
**B**. The spinal cord serves as the primary communication pathway — messages from the brain cannot reach the limbs if the pathway is damaged. \
**C**. The spinal cord produces the energy required for muscle movement, and injury stops this production. \
**D**. The spinal cord controls breathing, and without oxygen the muscles cannot function.

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**B.** The spinal cord is the main communication highway between the brain and the body. Damage blocks signals from reaching the limbs.</i>
</details>

### Question 2
> Coral reefs support approximately 25% of all marine species despite covering less than 1% of the ocean floor. When surrounding ocean temperatures rise by just one degree Celsius for several consecutive weeks, corals undergo a process called bleaching. What directly causes the corals to turn white during this event?

**A**. The corals produce a white protective mucus layer to shield themselves from heat. \
**B**. Harmful bacteria consume the corals' coloured tissues, leaving only the white calcium skeleton. \
**C**. The corals expel the microscopic algae living in their tissues, which provided both food and vibrant colour. \
**D**. Ocean acidification dissolves the coloured pigments in the coral's outer layer.

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**C.** Coral bleaching occurs when corals expel the symbiotic algae (zooxanthellae) living in their tissues, which provided food and colour.</i>
</details>

### Question 3
> A school installs solar panels on its rooftop. During a week-long period of heavy cloud cover, the panels produce significantly less electricity than usual. Which of the following best explains why?

**A**. Solar panels require direct sunlight because photovoltaic cells need photons to knock electrons loose and generate an electric current. \
**B**. Solar panels work by capturing heat from the sun, and clouds block the heat entirely. \
**C**. The panels contain liquid that needs to be warmed by the sun to create steam that turns a turbine. \
**D**. Heavy rain damages the surface of the panels, temporarily reducing their efficiency.

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**A.** Photovoltaic cells need photons from direct sunlight to knock electrons loose and generate electric current. Diffuse light through clouds produces fewer photons.</i>
</details>

### Question 6
> When Mount Vesuvius erupted in 79 AD, it buried the Roman city of Pompeii under ash and pumice. The molten rock that erupted from this volcano originated deep beneath the Earth's surface, where intense heat and pressure melted solid rock into a semi-liquid state. In which layer of the Earth does this melting primarily occur?

**A**. The crust — the thin outer layer that we walk on every day \
**B**. The mantle — the thick layer beneath the crust where heat and pressure generate magma \
**C**. The outer core — a layer of liquid iron and nickel surrounding the inner core \

    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>

### Question 11
> A rectangular community swimming pool measures 25 metres in length and 12 metres in width. A concrete walkway of uniform width 1.5 metres surrounds the pool on all four sides. What is the total area of the walkway?

**A**. 111 m² \
**B**. 117 m² \
**C**. 120 m² \
**D**. 123 m²

<details name="question-11">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**C.** Outer dimensions: (25+3)×(12+3) = 28×15 = 420 m². Pool: 25×12 = 300 m². Walkway: 420−300 = 120 m².</i>
</details>

### Question 12
> A bullet train (Shinkansen) in Japan travels at a speed of 300 km/h. A conventional train travelling the same route moves at 80 km/h. If both trains depart from Tokyo Station simultaneously at 8:00 AM heading toward Osaka along the same track, what distance will separate the two trains after 45 minutes?

**A**. 165 km \
**B**. 180 km \
**C**. 225 km \
**D**. 220 km

<details name="question-12">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**A.** After 45 min (0.75 h): Shinkansen 300×0.75 = 225 km. Conventional 80×0.75 = 60 km. Difference: 225−60 = 165 km.</i>
</details>

### Question 13
> Three different bus routes depart from the same station. Bus A departs every 12 minutes, Bus B every 18 minutes, and Bus C every 30 minutes. If all three buses depart together at 6:00 AM, at what time will they next depart simultaneously again?

**A**. 7:30 AM \
**B**. 8:00 AM \
**C**. 9:00 AM \
**D**. 8:30 AM

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**C.** LCM of 12, 18, 30 = 2²×3²×5 = 180 min = 3 hours. 6:00 AM + 3h = 9:00 AM.</i>
</details>

### Question 17
> A stationery store runs a promotion: "Buy 3 notebooks, Get 2 notebooks Free." Each notebook normally costs ₫12,000. A student spends exactly ₫60,000 on notebooks to take advantage of this offer. How many notebooks does the student receive in total?

**A**. 7 notebooks \
**B**. 8 notebooks \
**C**. 9 notebooks \
**D**. 10 notebooks

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**A.** Buy 3 get 2 free: each set of 3 paid = 5 received. With ₫60,000: buy 3 full-price (₫36,000) → get 2 free → 5 notebooks. Remaining ₫24,000 → buy 2 more. Total = 5 + 2 = 7 notebooks.</i>
</details>
