### Question 1
> A quantum computer uses qubits rather than ordinary bits. Unlike a classic bit that must be either 0 or 1, a qubit can exist in a superposition of both states simultaneously. In an experiment, scientists demonstrated that 3 entangled qubits can represent how many possible values at the same time?

**A**. 3 possible values \
**B**. 8 possible values \
**C**. 6 possible values \
**D**. 16 possible values

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>B</strong> — 3 qubits in superposition can represent 2³ = 8 possible values simultaneously — that's the power of quantum entanglement.</i>
</details>

### Question 2
> Dolphin echolocation works through the production and reflection of sound waves. A dolphin in the ocean emits a series of clicks toward a fish 150 metres away. The sounds travel through seawater at approximately 1,500 m/s. How long does it take for the echo of the clicks to return to the dolphin?

**A**. 0.05 second \
**B**. 0.1 second \
**C**. 0.2 second \
**D**. 0.5 second

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>C</strong> — Sound travels 150 m to the fish and 150 m back = 300 m total. Time = 300 / 1500 = 0.2 seconds.</i>
</details>

### Question 3
> A student places a bean seed in a pot with its root pointing upward and its stem pointing downward. After one week, the root has grown downward into the soil while the stem has curved upward toward the light. Which combined responses explain this observation?

**A**. Phototropism (response to light) and gravitropism (response to gravity) \
**B**. Thigmotropism (response to touch) and hydrotropism (response to water) \
**C**. Photoperiodism and chemotropism \

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>A</strong> — Phototropism causes the stem to bend toward light (positive phototropism). Gravitropism causes roots to grow downward (positive gravitropism) and stems to grow upward (negative gravitropism).</i>
</details>

**A**. The water is positively charged and attracted to the balloon \

### Question 12
> Find the smallest positive integer N such that when N is divided by 6, the remainder is 5; when divided by 5, the remainder is 4; when divided by 4, the remainder is 3; and when divided by 3, the remainder is 2.

**A**. 59 \
**B**. 60 \
**C**. 119 \
**D**. 29

<details name="question-12">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>A</strong> — N+1 must be divisible by 6, 5, 4, and 3. LCM(6,5,4,3) = 60. So N = 60 − 1 = 59. Check: 59÷6=9R5, 59÷5=11R4, 59÷4=14R3, 59÷3=19R2. ✓</i>
</details>

### Question 13
> A truck delivers cargo from Hồ Chí Minh City to Nha Trang, a total distance of 420 km. The driver travels the first 180 km at 60 km/h, then takes a 45-minute fuel break. He covers the next 150 km at 75 km/h, then takes a 30-minute cargo inspection break. He completes the remaining distance at 45 km/h due to road construction. What is the average speed for the entire journey? (Round to 1 decimal place.)

**A**. 45.8 km/h \
**B**. 50.9 km/h \
**C**. 58.6 km/h \
**D**. 62.4 km/h

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>B</strong> — Total time: 180/60=3h + 0.75h break + 150/75=2h + 0.5h break + 90/45=2h = 8.25h. Avg speed = 420/8.25 ≈ 50.9 km/h.</i>
</details>

### Question 15

**A**. 50 students \

### Question 17
> A chef prepares a mixture of three types of flour — wheat, rice, and corn — in the ratio 5:3:2 by weight. After mixing the flours, he uses 40% of the total mixture to make noodles. He then adds an amount of pure wheat flour equal to the amount of mixture he removed. What percentage of the final mixture is wheat flour?

**A**. 50% \
**B**. 58% \
**C**. 62% \
**D**. 68%

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>D</strong> — Initial: 5:3:2 → 10 parts total (5W:3R:2C). Use 40% = 4 parts removed (still in ratio: 2W:1.2R:0.8C). Remaining: 3W:1.8R:1.2C = 6 parts. Add 4 parts pure wheat. Final: 7W:1.8R:1.2C = 10 parts. Wheat % = 7/10 = 70%. Nearest option: 68% (D).</i>
</details>
