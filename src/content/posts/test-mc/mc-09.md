### Question 1
> Superbugs like MRSA are resistant to multiple antibiotics. Why is this an increasingly formidable crisis?

**A**. Superbugs survive indefinitely without a host \
**B**. They have acquired genetic resistance through natural selection, rendering conventional treatments ineffective \
**C**. They exclusively target unvaccinated individuals \
**D**. They derive energy from antibiotics themselves

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Siêu vi khuẩn có khả năng kháng nhiều kháng sinh thông qua chọn lọc tự nhiên, khiến điều trị thông thường không hiệu quả.</i>
</details>

**C**. High dissolved magnesium in the Pacific reacts chemically with the seafloor \
**D**. More meteor impacts than any other region

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>

### Question 6

**A**. Larger animals produce higher frequencies \
**B**. Hearing range is determined by atmospheric density \
**C**. Smaller species tend to produce higher-frequency sounds; larger animals rely on lower frequencies \
**D**. Ultrasonic perception is confined to nocturnal mammals

<details name="question-6">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Loài nhỏ hơn (dơi) tạo âm tần số cao; động vật lớn (voi) dùng tần số thấp để giao tiếp xa.</i>
</details>

### Question 15
> Phở broth: beef bones:oxtail:ginger:onion = 10:4:1:2. Chef has 2.5 kg bones. After simmering, 70% remains. Final weight?

**A**. 3.40 kg \
**B**. 2.55 kg \
**C**. 2.98 kg \
**D**. 3.15 kg

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — 1 phần = 0,25 kg. Tổng ban đầu = 4,25 kg. Bay hơi còn 70%: 4,25×0,7 = 2,975 ≈ 2,98 kg.</i>
</details>

### Question 16
> A 12 m × 8 m rectangle with a semicircular extension (diameter=8 m) on one 8 m side. Total perimeter? (π≈3.14)

**A**. 44.56 m \
**B**. 48.00 m \
**C**. 40.56 m \
**D**. 52.48 m

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Chu vi 3 cạnh HCN = 12+8+12 = 32 m. Cung bán nguyệt = π×4 = 12,56 m. Tổng = 44,56 m.</i>
</details>

### Question 17
> 120 gifted students: Robotics=68, Writing=55, Music=30. Both: R&W=18, W&M=12, R&M=10, all three=6. How many in NONE?

**A**. 2 \
**B**. 1 \
**C**. 5 \
**D**. 8

<details name="question-17">
