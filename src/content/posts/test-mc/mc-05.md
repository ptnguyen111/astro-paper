## I. SCIENCE

### Question 1

> A 10-year-old patient is experiencing difficulty with balance, coordination, and posture. A neurologist suspects damage to a specific brain region. Which part of the brain has MOST likely been affected?

**A**. Cerebrum (frontal lobe) \
**B**. Cerebellum — coordinates voluntary movements, balance, and posture \
**C**. Brainstem — controls breathing and heart rate \
**D**. Hypothalamus — regulates temperature, hunger, sleep

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Tiểu não kiểm soát thăng bằng, phối hợp cử động tự ý và tư thế.</i>
</details>

### Question 2

> A student pushes a wooden block across ice, carpet, and sandpaper, measuring the force needed each time. Which conclusion is MOST strongly supported?

**A**. Friction is greatest on ice \
**B**. Friction is identical on all surfaces \
**C**. Friction increases as the surface becomes rougher, requiring greater force \
**D**. Friction only occurs when the block starts moving

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Bề mặt càng nhám → ma sát càng lớn → cần nhiều lực hơn.</i>
</details>

### Question 3

> A student seals an empty plastic bottle at sea level (1013 mb) and travels to a mountain summit (700 mb). The bottle expands noticeably. What explains this?

**A**. Cold temperature contracts air inside \
**B**. Trapped air is at higher pressure than the surrounding atmosphere, pushing the walls outward \
**C**. Weaker gravity at altitude reduces the plastic's weight \
**D**. A chemical reaction produces extra gas

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Không khí bên trong chai có áp suất cao hơn khí quyển bên ngoài, đẩy thành chai phồng ra cho đến khi cân bằng.</i>
</details>

### Question 5

> At a convergent plate boundary where a dense oceanic plate meets a less dense continental plate, what geological phenomenon is MOST likely to occur?

**A**. Plates slide past each other, causing shallow earthquakes \
**B**. Oceanic plate subducts beneath continental plate, forming a deep trench and volcanic mountains \
**C**. Plates move apart, creating new oceanic crust \
**D**. Both plates rise upward, forming mountains without volcanoes

<details name="question-5">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Mảng đại dương chìm xuống dưới mảng lục địa → rãnh đại dương + núi lửa (hút chìm).</i>
</details>

**A**. 675,000 VND \

### Question 13

> A rectangular fish tank measures 80 cm × 40 cm × 50 cm high. It contains water to 30 cm. Minh pours an additional 24 litres. What is the new water height? (1 L = 1,000 cm³)

**A**. 35.5 cm \
**B**. 37.5 cm \
**C**. 42.0 cm \
**D**. 45.0 cm

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Đáy = 80×40 = 3.200 cm². Thêm 24.000 cm³ → cao thêm 24.000/3.200 = 7,5 cm. Mới = 30 + 7,5 = 37,5 cm.</i>
</details>

### Question 14

> Hà and Huy live 12 km apart on a straight road. At 8:00 AM, they cycle toward each other at 10 km/h and 14 km/h. At what time do they meet?

**A**. 8:24 AM \
**B**. 8:30 AM \
**C**. 8:36 AM \
**D**. 8:45 AM

<details name="question-14">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Tổng vận tốc = 24 km/h. Thời gian = 12/24 = 0,5 h = 30 ph. Gặp lúc 8:30 AM.</i>
</details>

### Question 15

> The ratio of apples to oranges is 3:5. There are 24 more oranges than apples. How many apples are there?

**A**. 12 \
**B**. 24 \
**C**. 36 \
**D**. 48

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Táo = 3x, cam = 5x. 5x−3x = 2x = 24 → x = 12. Táo = 3×12 = 36.</i>
</details>

### Question 16

> A rectangular garden is 12 m × 8 m. Inside it are a square flower bed (side 3 m) and a circular pond (radius 2 m, π ≈ 3.14). What area is NOT occupied by either?

**A**. 74.44 m² \
**B**. 73.44 m² \
**C**. 75.56 m² \
**D**. 77.44 m²

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Vườn = 96 m². Luống = 9 m². Ao = 12,56 m². Đã dùng = 21,56. Còn = 96 − 21,56 = 74,44 m².</i>
</details>

### Question 17

> Linh gives half her marbles to An, then 6 to her brother, and has 10 left. How many did she start with?

**A**. 28 \
**B**. 32 \
**C**. 36 \
**D**. 40

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — x/2 − 6 = 10 → x/2 = 16 → x = 32.</i>
</details>
