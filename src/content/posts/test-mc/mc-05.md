**A**. The cerebrum, specifically the frontal lobe responsible for decision-making and personality \
**B**. The cerebellum, which is primarily responsible for coordinating voluntary movements, balance, and posture \
**C**. The brainstem, which controls essential involuntary functions such as breathing and heart rate \
**D**. The hypothalamus, which regulates body temperature, hunger, and sleep cycles

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**B.** The cerebellum controls balance, coordination of voluntary movements, and posture. Damage to this area best explains the patient's symptoms (difficulty with balance, fine motor skills, posture).</i>

### Question 2
> A student conducts an experiment by pushing a wooden block across three different surfaces — ice, carpet, and sandpaper — and measures the force required to keep the block moving at a constant speed each time. Which conclusion is **MOST strongly** supported by this investigation?

**A**. Friction is greatest on ice because the surface is the smoothest and most slippery \
**B**. Friction is identical across all surfaces because the block and the applied force are the same \
**C**. Friction increases significantly as the surface becomes rougher, consequently requiring greater force to maintain motion \

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**C.** Rougher surfaces produce greater friction, requiring more force to maintain constant speed. Sandpaper > carpet > ice in friction.</i>

### Question 3
> A student seals an empty plastic bottle at sea level, where the atmospheric pressure is approximately 1013 millibars. She then travels to the summit of a high mountain where the atmospheric pressure is only 700 millibars. Upon arrival, she is astonished to find that the bottle has expanded noticeably. What principle **BEST** explains this phenomenon?

**A**. The lower temperature at high altitude causes the air molecules inside to contract and take up less space \
**B**. The air trapped inside the bottle is at higher pressure than the surrounding atmosphere, so it pushes the walls outward until equilibrium is approached \
**C**. Gravity is weaker at higher altitudes, consequently reducing the weight of the plastic and allowing it to stretch \
**D**. A chemical reaction between the plastic and the remaining oxygen produces additional gas inside the bottle

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**B.** The air sealed inside the bottle at sea level (1013 mb) is at higher pressure than the mountain-top atmosphere (700 mb), so it pushes the walls outward until internal and external pressures approach equilibrium.</i>
</details>

### Question 6
> A student constructs an electromagnet by wrapping a copper wire around an iron nail and connecting the ends of the wire to a battery. She wants to increase the strength of her electromagnet for a science fair project. Which modification would be **MOST effective** in achieving this goal?

**A**. Using a significantly shorter piece of copper wire to reduce resistance \
**B**. Reversing the positive and negative terminals of the battery \

### Question 13
> A rectangular fish tank measures 80 cm long, 40 cm wide, and 50 cm high. It currently contains water to a height of 30 cm. If Minh pours an additional 24 liters of water into the tank, what will be the new water height? *(1 liter = 1,000 cm³)*

**A**. 35.5 cm \
**B**. 37.5 cm \
**C**. 42.0 cm \
**D**. 45.0 cm

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**B.** Base area = 80 × 40 = 3,200 cm². Additional 24 L = 24,000 cm³. Height added = 24,000 / 3,200 = 7.5 cm. New height = 30 + 7.5 = **37.5 cm**.</i>
</details>

### Question 14

### Question 15
> In a fruit basket, the ratio of apples to oranges is **3 : 5**. There are 24 more oranges than apples. How many apples are in the basket?

**A**. 12 \
**B**. 24 \
**C**. 36 \
**D**. 48

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**C.** Let apples = 3x, oranges = 5x. Difference: 5x − 3x = 2x = 24 → x = 12. Apples = 3 × 12 = **36**.</i>
</details>

### Question 16
> A rectangular garden is 12 meters long and 8 meters wide. In the middle of the garden is a square flower bed with a side length of 3 meters and a circular pond with a radius of 2 meters. What is the area of the garden that is **NOT** occupied by either the flower bed or the pond? *(Use π ≈ 3.14)*

**A**. 74.44 m² \
**B**. 73.44 m² \
**C**. 75.56 m² \
**D**. 77.44 m²

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**A.** Garden area = 12 × 8 = 96 m². Square bed = 3 × 3 = 9 m². Circular pond = π × 2² ≈ 3.14 × 4 = 12.56 m². Occupied = 9 + 12.56 = 21.56 m². Unoccupied = 96 − 21.56 = **74.44 m²**.</i>
</details>

### Question 17
> Linh has a bag of marbles. She gives **half** of her marbles to her friend An. Then she gives 6 marbles to her younger brother. After that, she counts her remaining marbles and finds she has exactly 10 left. How many marbles did Linh have in the bag initially?

**A**. 28 \
**B**. 32 \
**C**. 36 \
**D**. 40

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">**B.** Let x = initial marbles. After giving half: x/2 left. Then gives 6: x/2 − 6. Equation: x/2 − 6 = 10 → x/2 = 16 → x = **32**.</i>
</details>
