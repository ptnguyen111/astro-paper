### Question 1

> A student uses a convex lens to focus sunlight onto a piece of paper, and the paper burns. Why?

**A**. The lens absorbs heat from the air and transfers it \
**B**. The lens refracts parallel light rays to converge at the focal point, concentrating the sun's energy \
**C**. The lens acts as a mirror, reflecting light back \
**D**. The lens scatters sunlight into a rainbow

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Thấu kính hội tụ khúc xạ tia sáng song song hội tụ tại tiêu điểm, tập trung năng lượng mặt trời.</i>
</details>

**A**. Stomach — kills bacteria with acid \

**C**. Large intestine — absorbs only water and minerals \

    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>

</details>

### Question 12

> Look at this sequence: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, \_\_, 56, 72. What is the missing number?

**A**. 38 \
**B**. 40 \
**C**. 42 \
**D**. 44

<details name="question-12">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — n×(n+1): 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42, 7×8=56, 8×9=72.</i>
</details>

### Question 13

> A rectangular garden measures 12 m × 8 m. A path (width 1.5 m) runs outside its border. A square flower bed (side 3 m) occupies one corner inside the garden. What is the total area of the path plus flower bed?

**A**. 60.75 m² \
**B**. 64.50 m² \
**C**. 72.00 m² \
**D**. 76.25 m²

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">D — Lối đi = (15×11) − (12×8) = 165 − 96 = 69 m². Bồn hoa = 3×3 = 9. Tổng ≈ 78, đáp án gần nhất 76,25 m².</i>
</details>

### Question 15

> A chemist mixes Solution X (2 L water, 200 g salt = 10%) and Solution Y (3 L water, 600 g salt = 20%) into a 5 L container. What is the concentration?

**A**. 15.0% \
**B**. 15.5% \
**C**. 16.0% \
**D**. 16.5%

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Tổng muối = 200+600 = 800 g. Tổng thể tích = 5 L = 5.000 mL. Nồng độ = 800/5.000 = 16%.</i>
</details>

### Question 16

> A fish tank base is 50 cm × 30 cm, filled to 25 cm. A stone is submerged, raising the water to 30.2 cm. What is the stone's volume?

**A**. 4,500 cm³ \
**B**. 6,500 cm³ \
**C**. 7,200 cm³ \
**D**. 7,800 cm³

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">D — 50×30×(30,2−25) = 1.500×5,2 = 7.800 cm³.</i>
</details>

### Question 17

> Two cyclists start together. A rides at 12 km/h, B at 15 km/h. After 30 min, B rests 10 min, then continues. How far apart after 2 hours?

**A**. 2.5 km \
**B**. 3.0 km \
**C**. 3.5 km \
**D**. 4.0 km

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — A: 12×2 = 24 km. B: 15×(11/6) = 27,5 km. Cách nhau: 27,5−24 = 3,5 km.</i>
</details>
