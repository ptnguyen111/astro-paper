### Question 1
> The human heart has four chambers that work together to pump blood. Which chamber receives oxygen-rich blood from the lungs before it is pumped to the rest of the body?

**A**. Right atrium — receives deoxygenated blood from the body \
**B**. Right ventricle — pumps deoxygenated blood to the lungs \
**C**. Left atrium — receives oxygenated blood from the pulmonary veins \
**D**. Left ventricle — pumps oxygenated blood to the body, but does not receive it directly from the lungs

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><strong>C</strong>. The left atrium receives oxygen-rich blood from the lungs via the pulmonary veins.</i>
</details>

**A**. If the hall is filled with air at normal temperature \
**B**. If the hall is completely evacuated of air (a vacuum) \

### Question 4
> A gardener plants identical bean seeds in four pots under different conditions and observes them for one week:
>
> • Pot A: water + sunlight → sprouts grow tall and green
>
> • Pot B: water + no sunlight → sprouts grow tall but pale yellow
>
> • Pot C: no water + sunlight → no growth
>
> • Pot D: no water + no sunlight → no growth
>
> What conclusion can the gardener draw from this experiment?

**A**. Seeds need both water and sunlight to germinate, after which sunlight is needed for healthy green growth. \
**B**. Seeds need water but not sunlight to germinate; however, sunlight is necessary for the plant to develop chlorophyll and turn green. \
**C**. Seeds need sunlight but not water to germinate. \
**D**. The experiment is inconclusive because the pots should have been kept at different temperatures.

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><strong>B</strong>. Pots C and D show seeds need water to germinate. Pot B shows seeds can sprout without light, but light is needed for chlorophyll (green colour).</i>
</details>

### Question 11
> A construction mixture of cement and sand weighs 120 kg. The ratio of cement to sand by weight is 3 : 5. If 20 kg of sand is added to the mixture, what is the new ratio of cement to sand?

**A**. 3 : 7 \
**B**. 9 : 19 \
**C**. 9 : 25 \
**D**. 3 : 10

<details name="question-11">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><strong>B</strong>. Original: cement = (3/8)×120 = 45 kg, sand = (5/8)×120 = 75 kg. Add 20 kg sand → sand = 95 kg. New ratio = 45:95 = 9:19.</i>
</details>

### Question 13
> Three traffic lights at a busy junction change colour at different intervals:
>
> • Light A turns red every 60 seconds
>
> • Light B turns red every 75 seconds
>
> • Light C turns red every 90 seconds
>
> If all three turn red simultaneously at exactly 7:00:00 AM, at what time will they next all turn red together?

**A**. 7:12:00 AM \
**B**. 7:15:00 AM \
**C**. 7:18:00 AM \
**D**. 7:30:00 AM

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><strong>B</strong>. LCM of 60, 75, 90: 2²×3²×5² = 4×9×25 = 900 seconds = 15 minutes. 7:00 AM + 15 min = 7:15 AM.</i>
</details>

### Question 14
> A rectangular school playground measures 50 metres in length and 30 metres in width. A concrete path of uniform width 2 metres runs along the inside edge of all four sides of the playground (so the path forms a frame, and the grass area is the inner rectangle). What is the area of the concrete path?

**A**. 284 m² \
**B**. 304 m² \
**C**. 320 m² \
**D**. 344 m²

<details name="question-14">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><strong>B</strong>. Outer = 50×30 = 1,500. Inner = (50−4)×(30−4) = 46×26 = 1,196. Path = 1,500−1,196 = 304 m².</i>
</details>

### Question 16
> The ages of a grandfather, his son, and his grandson are in the ratio 10 : 5 : 2. The sum of their three ages is 102 years. In how many years from now will the grandson be exactly half the age of his father?

**A**. 4 years \
**B**. 6 years \
**C**. 8 years \
**D**. 10 years

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key"><strong>B</strong>. Ratio 10:5:2. Sum = 17x = 102 → x = 6. Ages: 60, 30, 12. In y years: 12+y = (30+y)/2 → 24+2y = 30+y → y = 6 years.</i>
</details>

**D**. Sự hài lòng khi tìm được tri kỉ giữa núi rừng; "ta với ta" là tình bạn tri âm giữa tác giả và một người bạn đồng hành.

<details name="question-19">
