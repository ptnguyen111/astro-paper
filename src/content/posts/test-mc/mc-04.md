## I. SCIENCE

### Question 1
> The human heart has four chambers that work together to pump blood. Which chamber receives oxygen-rich blood from the lungs before it is pumped to the rest of the body?

**A**. Right atrium — receives deoxygenated blood from the body \
**B**. Right ventricle — pumps deoxygenated blood to the lungs \
**C**. Left atrium — receives oxygenated blood from the pulmonary veins \
**D**. Left ventricle — pumps oxygenated blood to the body, but does not receive it directly from the lungs

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Tâm nhĩ trái nhận máu giàu oxy từ phổi qua các tĩnh mạch phổi.</i>
</details>

### Question 2
> A student often feels exhausted at school, struggles to concentrate, and catches colds frequently. A doctor recommends eating more eggs, fish, and beans. Which nutrient is the student most likely deficient in?

**A**. Carbohydrates — found in rice and bread, provides quick energy \
**B**. Vitamin C — found in citrus fruits, helps prevent infections \
**C**. Protein — found in eggs and fish, essential for immune function and tissue repair \
**D**. Calcium — found in milk and dairy, strengthens bones

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Mệt mỏi, kém tập trung, hay cảm lạnh → thiếu protein. Trứng, cá, đậu giàu protein.</i>
</details>

### Question 3
> A musician plays a note on a violin in a concert hall. The sound reaches a person standing 100 metres away. In which situation would the sound reach that person the fastest?

**A**. If the hall is filled with air at normal temperature \
**B**. If the hall is completely evacuated of air (a vacuum) \
**C**. If the person puts their ear against a long steel railing connected to the stage \
**D**. If the person is underwater in a pool 100 metres from the stage

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">C — Âm thanh truyền nhanh nhất qua chất rắn. Lan can thép dẫn âm nhanh hơn không khí hoặc nước.</i>
</details>

### Question 4
> A gardener plants identical bean seeds in four pots under different conditions and observes them for one week: Pot A (water + sunlight) → sprouts grow tall and green; Pot B (water + no sunlight) → sprouts grow tall but pale yellow; Pot C (no water + sunlight) → no growth; Pot D (no water + no sunlight) → no growth. What conclusion can be drawn?

**A**. Seeds need both water and sunlight to germinate \
**B**. Seeds need water but not sunlight to germinate; sunlight is necessary for chlorophyll development \
**C**. Seeds need sunlight but not water to germinate \
**D**. The experiment is inconclusive

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Nước đủ cho nảy mầm; ánh sáng cần cho diệp lục (màu xanh) phát triển.</i>
</details>

### Question 6
> Earth's axis is tilted at an angle of about 23.5 degrees relative to its orbital plane. What would likely happen if Earth's axis had no tilt at all (0 degrees)?

**A**. The equator would become much colder than the poles \
**B**. No distinct seasons; every region would experience roughly the same daylight throughout the year \
**C**. The length of a day would double everywhere on Earth \
**D**. The Sun would rise in the west and set in the east

<details name="question-6">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Độ nghiêng trục Trái Đất tạo ra các mùa. Không có độ nghiêng → không có mùa rõ rệt.</i>
</details>

**A**. The Amazon flows entirely through Brazil \

</details>

### Question 11
> A construction mixture of cement and sand weighs 120 kg. The ratio of cement to sand by weight is 3:5. If 20 kg of sand is added, what is the new ratio?

**A**. 3:7 \
**B**. 9:19 \
**C**. 9:25 \
**D**. 3:10

<details name="question-11">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Cement = (3/8)×120 = 45, cát = 75. Thêm 20 cát → cát=95. Tỉ lệ mới 45:95 = 9:19.</i>
</details>

### Question 13
> Three traffic lights change colour at different intervals: Light A every 60 seconds, Light B every 75 seconds, Light C every 90 seconds. If all three turn red simultaneously at 7:00:00 AM, when will they next all turn red together?

**A**. 7:12 AM \
**B**. 7:15 AM \
**C**. 7:18 AM \
**D**. 7:30 AM

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — BCNN(60,75,90) = 900 giây = 15 phút. 7:00 + 15 ph = 7:15 AM.</i>
</details>

### Question 14
> A rectangular school playground measures 50 m by 30 m. A concrete path of uniform width 2 metres runs along the inside edge of all four sides. What is the area of the path?

**A**. 284 m² \
**B**. 304 m² \
**C**. 320 m² \
**D**. 344 m²

<details name="question-14">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Ngoài = 50×30 = 1.500. Trong = 46×26 = 1.196. Đường = 1.500−1.196 = 304 m².</i>
</details>

### Question 16
> The ages of a grandfather, his son, and his grandson are in the ratio 10:5:2. The sum of their ages is 102 years. In how many years will the grandson be exactly half the age of his father?

**A**. 4 years \
**B**. 6 years \
**C**. 8 years \
**D**. 10 years

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">B — Tuổi: 60, 30, 12. Sau y năm: 12+y = (30+y)/2 → 24+2y = 30+y → y = 6 năm.</i>
</details>

### Question 17
> In a school talent show, 100 students participated. Data: 48 sing, 39 dance, 27 play instrument, 14 sing+dance, 9 sing+instrument, 8 dance+instrument, 5 do all three. How many students do NONE of these activities?

**A**. 12 \
**B**. 16 \
**C**. 18 \
**D**. 20

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">A — Venn: chỉ Hát=30, chỉ Nhảy=22, chỉ Nhạc=15, H∩N=9, H∩Nc=4, N∩Nc=3, cả ba=5. Tổng=88. Không=100−88=12.</i>
</details>
