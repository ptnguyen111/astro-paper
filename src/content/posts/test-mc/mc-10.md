> A student places a potted bean plant on its side inside a dark box with a small opening on one wall that admits a narrow beam of light. Which combination of tropisms explains these observations?

**A**. Negative phototropism (stem away from light) and positive gravitropism (roots toward gravity) \
**B**. Positive phototropism (stem toward light) and positive gravitropism (roots toward gravity) \
**C**. Positive phototropism (stem toward light) and negative gravitropism (roots away from gravity) \

    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>

### Question 13
> In a chemistry experiment, a student prepares a solution by mixing 200 millilitres of liquid A with a concentration of 15% salt and 300 millilitres of liquid B with a concentration of 25% salt. She then adds 500 millilitres of pure water to the mixture. What is the approximate salt concentration of the final solution?

**A**. 7.5% \
**B**. 9% \
**C**. 10.5% \
**D**. 13%

<details name="question-13">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>C</strong> — Salt from A: 200 × 15% = 30 mL. Salt from B: 300 × 25% = 75 mL. Total salt = 105 mL. Total volume = 200+300+500 = 1000 mL. Concentration = 105/1000 = 10.5%.</i>
</details>

### Question 14
> A carpenter has built a cuboid-shaped wooden block measuring 5 cm in length, 4 cm in width, and 3 cm in height from small unit cubes of 1 cm × 1 cm × 1 cm. He paints the entire outer surface of the large block blue. After the paint dries, he disassembles the block back into individual unit cubes. How many of the unit cubes will have exactly two faces painted blue?

**A**. 12 cubes \
**B**. 18 cubes \
**C**. 20 cubes \
**D**. 24 cubes

<details name="question-14">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>D</strong> — Cuboid 5×4×3. Two painted faces = edge cubes minus corners. Length edges: 4 × (5-2) = 12. Width edges: 4 × (4-2) = 8. Height edges: 4 × (3-2) = 4. Total = 12+8+4 = 24 cubes.</i>
</details>

### Question 15
> A cyclist rides from Town X to Town Y at an average speed of 15 km/h. She immediately returns from Town Y back to Town X along the same route at an average speed of 10 km/h. The entire round trip takes exactly 5 hours. What is the distance, in kilometres, between Town X and Town Y?

**A**. 25 km \
**B**. 30 km \
**C**. 35 km \
**D**. 40 km

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>B</strong> — Let d = distance. Time X→Y = d/15. Time Y→X = d/10. Total: d/15 + d/10 = 5 → (2d+3d)/30 = 5 → 5d = 150 → d = 30 km.</i>
</details>

### Question 16
> A farmer has a rectangular plot of land measuring 22 metres by 18 metres. He plants vegetables in a rectangular area at the centre, leaving a flower bed of uniform width around all four sides. The area of the vegetable patch is 252 square metres. What is the uniform width of the flower bed?

**A**. 1 metre \
**B**. 2 metres \
**C**. 3 metres \
**D**. 4 metres

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>B</strong> — Plot 22×18. Vegetable area = (22-2w)(18-2w) = 252. Expand: 4w² - 80w + 396 = 252 → 4w² - 80w + 144 = 0 → w² - 20w + 36 = 0 → (w-2)(w-18) = 0 → w = 2 m.</i>
</details>

### Question 17
> An architect designs a decorative garden pond that consists of a rectangle 6 metres long and 4 metres wide, with a semicircle attached to each of the two shorter sides (the 4-metre sides). The semicircles open outward from the rectangle. Using π ≈ 3.14, what is the total area of the pond in square metres?

**A**. 36.56 m² \
**B**. 49.12 m² \
**C**. 57.68 m² \
**D**. 61.44 m²

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show awnswer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>A</strong> — Rectangle area = 6×4 = 24 m². Two semicircles (diameter 4 m, radius 2 m) = one full circle = π×2² = 3.14×4 = 12.56 m². Total = 24 + 12.56 = 36.56 m².</i>
