### Question 1

**Q1 (Space Science — Moon Phases) — 2 điểm**

Mai observes the Moon for two weeks. In the first week, she sees a thin crescent in the western sky just after sunset. A week later, she sees half of the Moon clearly lit at dusk. Her friend Nam says: "The Moon changes shape because the Earth casts a shadow on it."

Is Nam correct? Which explanation is CORRECT?

**A**. Nam is correct — the Earth's shadow covers different parts of the Moon over time. \
**B**. Nam is wrong — the Moon orbits the Earth, so we see different parts of its sunlit half from Earth. \
**C**. Nam is correct — clouds cover parts of the Moon, making it appear to change shape. \
**D**. Nam is wrong — the Moon gives off dimmer light on some days and brighter light on others.

Khi Mặt Trăng quay quanh Trái Đất, góc nhìn từ Trái Đất đến nửa được chiếu sáng thay đổi, tạo ra các pha. Đáp án A sai vì bóng Trái Đất chỉ che Mặt Trăng trong nguyệt thực, không phải là nguyên nhân của các pha thường xuyên.</i>

### Question 2

**Q2 (Renewable Energy — Solar Power) — 2 điểm**

Minh's father installs solar panels on their roof. On a hot sunny day, the panels produce 5 kWh of electricity. On a cloudy winter day, they produce only 0.8 kWh. At night, they produce 0 kWh. Minh's father says: "Solar panels can completely replace the electricity grid for our family."

Is his statement accurate?

**A**. Yes — solar panels produce clean energy both day and night. \
**B**. No — solar panels only work when there is sunlight, and output depends on weather, so backup power is needed. \
**C**. Yes — with a battery, excess energy from sunny days can be stored for night and cloudy days. \
**D**. No — solar panels are not efficient enough to power a home.

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">B — Nhận định của bố Minh không chính xác. Pin mặt trời chỉ tạo ra điện khi có ánh sáng mặt trời. Sản lượng thay đổi theo cường độ ánh sáng. Vì vậy, pin mặt trời không thể thay thế hoàn toàn điện lưới nếu không có hệ thống lưu trữ năng lượng hoặc nguồn dự phòng. Đáp án C có vẻ hợp lý nhưng không được đề cập trong câu hỏi — câu hỏi chỉ hỏi về nhận định của bố Minh.</i>

### Question 3

**Q3 (Animal Biology — Regeneration) — 2 điểm**

Lan keeps a snail in a glass box. One day, she notices that one of the snail's two eye stalks is shorter than the other. Her uncle says: "Don't worry — the snail will grow a new eye. If you cut the snail in half, it will become two snails." Lan's science book says snails can regenerate their eyes but cannot regrow their entire body if cut in half.

Which statement is CORRECT?

**A**. Lan's uncle is completely wrong — snails cannot regenerate any body parts. \
**B**. Both of his ideas are wrong — snails can only regenerate their shells. \
**C**. The first idea (snail regrows an eye) is correct, but the second (cut in half → two snails) is wrong. \

### Question 4

**Q4 (Simple Chemistry — Rust Prevention) — 2 điểm**

An places three identical iron nails in three different conditions:

- **Nail 1:** In a dry glass jar with a silica gel pack (moisture absorber).
- **Nail 2:** Fully submerged in cooled boiled water, with a layer of cooking oil on top.
- **Nail 3:** In a glass jar with normal air.

After one week, Nail 3 is rusty, but Nails 1 and 2 are not. What does this experiment prove?

**A**. Iron only rusts in salt water. \
**B**. Iron needs both oxygen and water to rust; removing either one prevents rust. \
**C**. Iron rusts because of bacteria in the air — Nails 1 and 2 were protected from bacteria. \
**D**. Iron only rusts when cooking oil or fat is present.

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">B — Đinh 1 có không khí (oxy) nhưng khô (silica gel hút ẩm) → không gỉ. Đinh 2 có nước nhưng đã đun sôi (loại bỏ phần lớn oxy hòa tan) và lớp dầu ngăn oxy từ không khí hòa tan vào nước → không gỉ. Đinh 3 có cả oxy và nước trong không khí ẩm → bị gỉ. Sắt cần cả oxy và nước để bị gỉ.</i>
</details>

### Question 5

**Q5 (Tropism — Plant Growth) — 2 điểm**

A student places a newly sprouted bean plant in a cardboard box with a small hole on one side. After 5 days, the bean plant has grown curved toward the light coming through the hole. The student's friend says: "The plant is reaching toward light because it needs sunlight to make food."

What is INCOMPLETE or INCORRECT about this explanation?

**A**. The friend is completely right — plants need light for photosynthesis to make food. \
**B**. The plant is not reaching toward light — it is responding to gravity, not light. \
**C**. Plants grow toward light because growth hormone (auxin) concentrates on the dark side, making cells there grow faster; the plant uses photosynthesis for food, but the curved growth is a separate response. \
**D**. Plants respond to the heat from light, not the light itself.

<details name="question-5">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">C — Cây mọc cong về phía ánh sáng là do hiện tượng hướng sáng dương (positive phototropism). Hoóc-môn auxin tập trung nhiều ở phía tối (xa ánh sáng), kích thích tế bào ở phía đó dài ra nhanh hơn, làm thân cây cong về phía ánh sáng. Bạn của em ấy nói đúng về nhu cầu ánh sáng của cây nhưng giải thích chưa đầy đủ về cơ chế mọc cong.</i>
</details>

### Question 6

**Q6 (Gravity & Weight) — 2 điểm**

A student has a mass of 36 kg. She learns that gravity on Mars is about 1/3 of Earth's, and on Jupiter it is about 2.5 times Earth's. She says: "If I go to Mars, my mass will be only 12 kg because gravity is weaker. If I go to Jupiter, my mass will be 90 kg."

How would you explain this to her?

**A**. She is correct — mass changes with gravity. \
**B**. She is correct about Mars but wrong about Jupiter. \
**C**. She is wrong — mass always stays the same (36 kg) anywhere; only weight (gravitational force) changes. \
**D**. She is wrong — on Mars her mass would be 108 kg because weaker gravity makes objects heavier.

<details name="question-6">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">C — Khối lượng (mass) là lượng vật chất của vật — không thay đổi dù ở bất kỳ đâu (vẫn là 36 kg). Trọng lượng (weight) là lực hấp dẫn tác dụng lên vật — thay đổi theo trọng lực. Trên Sao Hỏa trọng lượng = 36 × 1/3 = 12 kg, trên Sao Mộc trọng lượng = 36 × 2,5 = 90 kg. Nhưng khối lượng KHÔNG đổi.</i>
</details>

### Question 9

**Q9 (World Geography — Time Zones) — 2 điểm**

A plane departs from Hanoi (Vietnam, UTC+7) at 9:00 AM Tuesday (Hanoi time) and flies to Tokyo (Japan, UTC+9). The flight time is 5 hours. What time and day does it arrive in Tokyo?

**A**. 2:00 PM Tuesday (9 + 5 = 14, ignoring time zone difference). \
**B**. 4:00 PM Tuesday (9 AM Hanoi = 11 AM Tokyo; 11 + 5 = 4 PM). \
**C**. 9:00 PM Tuesday (wrong calculation of time zone difference). \
**D**. 7:00 AM Tuesday (confusing calculation).

<details name="question-9">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">B — Hà Nội UTC+7, Tokyo UTC+9 → chênh lệch +2 giờ. Khi ở Hà Nội là 9 giờ sáng, ở Tokyo là 9 + 2 = 11 giờ sáng cùng ngày (thứ Ba). Thời gian bay 5 giờ → đến Tokyo lúc 11 + 5 = 16 giờ thứ Ba.</i>
</details>

### Question 15

**Q15 (Measurement — Unit Conversion) — 2 điểm**

A rectangular garden has a length of 12 m and a width of 8 m. You want to cover the garden with square paving stones that are 40 cm × 40 cm. How many paving stones do you need?

**A**. 240 stones \
**B**. 600 stones \
**C**. 60 stones \
**D**. 24 stones

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>
    <i class="answer-key">B — Diện tích vườn: 12 × 8 = 96 m². Kích thước đá lát: 40 cm = 0,4 m. Diện tích một viên đá: 0,4 × 0,4 = 0,16 m². Số viên: 96 ÷ 0,16 = 600 viên. Cách khác: 12 ÷ 0,4 = 30 viên chiều dài; 8 ÷ 0,4 = 20 viên chiều rộng; 30 × 20 = 600 viên.</i>
</details>

### Question 17

**Q17 (Calendar / Date Logic) — 2 điểm**

If January 1, 2026 is a Thursday, what day of the week is March 8, 2026 (International Women's Day)? (Note: 2026 is not a leap year; January has 31 days, February has 28 days, March has 31 days.)

**B**. Sunday \
**C**. Monday \
**D**. Tuesday

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>
