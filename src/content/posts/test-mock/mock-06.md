**Question 1.** Mai rubs a balloon on her wool sweater for 10 seconds. She then holds the balloon near her hair. Her hair stands up and sticks to the balloon. After 2 minutes, her hair slowly falls back down. Which process best explains what happened at each stage?

**A**. The balloon gained heat from rubbing → hot air pushed the hair up → the air cooled after 2 minutes \
**B**. Rubbing transferred electrons → opposite charges attracted the hair → charges slowly leaked away \
**C**. The wool sweater pushed tiny hairs onto the balloon → gravity pulled them back after 2 minutes \
**D**. Static friction made the balloon magnetic → hair attracted to magnetic field → field faded away

<details name="question-1">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>B</strong>. Rubbing transfers electrons (static electricity). Opposite charges attract → hair sticks. Charges slowly leak away (discharge) → hair falls. (2đ)</i>
</details>

**Question 2.** A large steel ship weighs 50,000 tons and floats on the ocean. A small steel nail weighs 5 grams and sinks immediately when dropped into water. Both objects are made of the same material. What is the main reason the ship floats but the nail sinks?

**A**. The ship is bigger, so water pushes harder on its surface compared to the nail \
**B**. The ship is shaped to push aside enough water to make an upward force equal to its weight \
**C**. Steel becomes lighter when it is shaped into a large flat object \
**D**. Saltwater in the ocean is thicker and can hold up heavy steel objects

<details name="question-2">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>B</strong>. A ship floats because its shape displaces a large volume of water. The buoyant force equals the weight of water displaced. The nail sinks because it displaces too little water to support its weight. (2đ)</i>
</details>

**Question 3.** In science class, Nam hangs a heavy metal weight on a 50 cm string. He counts 20 swings in 30 seconds. He then replaces the weight with a lighter plastic one on the same string. When he counts again, how many swings will he most likely see in 30 seconds?

**A**. Less than 20 swings — lighter objects cannot swing as fast as heavy ones \
**B**. About 20 swings — the string length controls the speed, not the weight \
**C**. More than 30 swings — light plastic moves much faster than heavy metal \
**D**. Exactly 10 swings — half the weight means half the number of swings

<details name="question-3">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>B</strong>. Pendulum period depends on string length, not weight. Galileo's discovery: as long as the string is the same length, the swing time stays the same regardless of the mass. (2đ)</i>
</details>

**Question 4.** Lan mixes 3 spoons of salt into a glass of warm water until the salt disappears. She pours the salty water into a shallow bowl and leaves it on the windowsill in the sun. After 3 days, the water is gone and white crystals cover the bottom of the bowl. What explains the appearance of these white crystals?

**A**. The salt was destroyed by sunlight and turned into a white powder \
**B**. The water evaporated, leaving behind the salt that had dissolved in it \
**C**. The warm air outside mixed with the water and created new salt crystals \
**D**. The salt melted from the heat and then cooled down into solid crystals

<details name="question-4">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>B</strong>. The salt dissolved in water. When water evaporated (turned into vapor), the salt could no longer stay dissolved and formed solid crystals again. This is a physical change, not a chemical one. (2đ)</i>
</details>

**Question 15.** A rectangular tank is 40 cm long, 30 cm wide, and 25 cm tall. The tank is filled with water to a height of 20 cm. Minh then places a heavy metal cube with 10 cm sides into the water. The cube sinks to the bottom and is completely covered by water. By how many centimetres does the water level rise?

**A**. 0.5 cm \
**B**. 0.75 cm \
**C**. 0.83 cm \
**D**. 1.25 cm

<details name="question-15">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>C</strong>.<br>
    Volume of cube = 10³ = 1,000 cm³.<br>
    Tank base area = 40 × 30 = 1,200 cm².<br>
    Rise = 1,000 ÷ 1,200 = 0.833... ≈ <strong>0.83 cm</strong>. (2đ)</i>
</details>

**Question 16.** A glass contains 4 parts of orange juice and 3 parts of water. Anh drinks half of the mixture. She then fills the glass back up with pure orange juice. What percentage of the final mixture is orange juice?

**A**. 57.1% \
**B**. 64.3% \
**C**. 71.4% \
**D**. 78.6%

<details name="question-16">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>D</strong>.<br>
    Initial: 4 orange + 3 water = 7 parts. Orange = 4/7 ≈ 57.14%.<br>
    Drink half: remove 3.5 parts. Remaining: 2 orange + 1.5 water.<br>
    Fill to 7: add 3.5 orange. Final: 2+3.5 = 5.5 orange, 1.5 water.<br>
    Orange = 5.5/7 = 78.57% ≈ <strong>78.6%</strong>. (2đ)</i>
</details>

**Question 17.** Two equal squares are placed so that they overlap. Each square has a side length of 6 cm. The overlapping area is a rectangle measuring 4 cm by 3 cm. What is the total area of the shape formed by the two squares together?

**A**. 60 cm² \
**B**. 66 cm² \
**C**. 72 cm² \
**D**. 84 cm²

<details name="question-17">
    <summary><i>Show answer key</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Đáp án: <strong>A</strong>.<br>
    Total area of 2 squares = 2 × (6×6) = 72 cm².<br>
    Overlapping area = 4×3 = 12 cm² (counted twice in the total).<br>
    Actual area = 72 − 12 = <strong>60 cm²</strong>. (2đ)</i>
</details>

### Bài 1. (3,0 điểm)

Một cửa hàng bán trái cây trong một tuần có doanh thu tổng cộng là 12.600.000 đồng. Biểu đồ hình tròn dưới đây cho biết tỉ lệ doanh thu của từng loại quả. Biết rằng:

- Ổi chiếm 20%
- thanh long chiếm 35%
- cam chiếm 15%
- nho chiếm 10%
- còn lại là xoài

**a) (1 điểm)** Doanh thu của xoài chiếm bao nhiêu phần trăm?

**b) (1 điểm)** Doanh thu của xoài là bao nhiêu tiền?

**c) (1 điểm)** Doanh thu của ổi và cam nhiều hơn doanh thu của nho bao nhiêu tiền?

<details name="math-1-a">
    <summary><i>Show answer key — a)</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Xoài = 100% − 20% − 35% − 15% − 10% = <strong>20%</strong> (1đ)</i>
</details>

<details name="math-1-b">
    <summary><i>Show answer key — b)</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Doanh thu xoài = 12.600.000 × 20% = <strong>2.520.000 đồng</strong> (1đ)</i>
</details>

<details name="math-1-c">
    <summary><i>Show answer key — c)</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Ổi + cam = 20% + 15% = 35%. Nho = 10%.<br>Chênh lệch = (35% − 10%) × 12.600.000 = 25% × 12.600.000 = <strong>3.150.000 đồng</strong> (1đ)</i>
</details>

### Bài 4. (6,0 điểm)

Có hai thùng nước hình hộp chữ nhật:

- **Thùng A:** dài 50 cm, rộng 40 cm, cao 50 cm. Hiện thùng A đang chứa đầy nước.
- **Thùng B:** dài 80 cm, rộng 50 cm, cao 45 cm. Hiện thùng B đang rỗng.

Người ta đổ nước từ thùng A vào thùng B. Sau khi đổ, mực nước ở thùng A cao gấp 2 lần mực nước ở thùng B.

**a) (1,5 điểm)** Tính thể tích của thùng A và thùng B.

**b) (0,5 điểm)** Tìm diện tích đáy của mỗi thùng.

**c) (2,5 điểm)** Tính chiều cao mực nước ở thùng B sau khi đổ.

**d) (0,5 điểm)** Tính chiều cao mực nước ở thùng A sau khi đổ.

**e) (1,0 điểm)** Hỏi thể tích nước còn lại trong thùng A là bao nhiêu lít?

_(Biết rằng: 1 lít = 1.000 cm³)_

<details name="math-4-a">
    <summary><i>Show answer key — a)</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">V_A = 50 × 40 × 50 = <strong>100.000 cm³</strong><br>V_B = 80 × 50 × 45 = <strong>180.000 cm³</strong> (1,5đ)</i>
</details>

<details name="math-4-b">
    <summary><i>Show answer key — b)</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">S_A = 50 × 40 = <strong>2.000 cm²</strong><br>S_B = 80 × 50 = <strong>4.000 cm²</strong> (0,5đ)</i>
</details>

<details name="math-4-c">
    <summary><i>Show answer key — c)</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">
        Gọi h_B là chiều cao mực nước ở thùng B sau khi đổ.<br>
        Theo đề bài: h_A = 2 × h_B (mực nước A cao gấp 2 lần mực nước B)<br><br>
        Thể tích nước còn lại trong A: 2.000 × h_A = 2.000 × 2h_B = 4.000 × h_B<br>
        Thể tích nước trong B: 4.000 × h_B<br><br>
        Tổng thể tích nước (bảo toàn):<br>
        4.000h_B + 4.000h_B = 100.000<br>
        8.000h_B = 100.000<br>
        h_B = 100.000 : 8.000 = <strong>12,5 cm</strong> (2,5đ)
    </i>
</details>

<details name="math-4-d">
    <summary><i>Show answer key — d)</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">h_A = 2 × h_B = 2 × 12,5 = <strong>25 cm</strong> (0,5đ)</i>
</details>

<details name="math-4-e">
    <summary><i>Show answer key — e)</i></summary>    
    <i class="answer-key">Thể tích nước còn lại trong A: V_còn = S_A × h_A = 2.000 × 25 = 50.000 cm³ = <strong>50 lít</strong> (1,0đ)</i>
</details>
